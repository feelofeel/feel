# The eel mythology

**Brand-internal.** This file powers visuals, naming, and voice. It is not product documentation; nothing in it is required to understand or use FEEL, and none of it appears in the foundation docs or user-facing reference material. Users who just want a good documentation system never need to meet this file.

## The cosmology (private)

`feelofeel` reads two ways. Most people read *feel-o-feel*. The author reads ***feel of eel*** — the eel is the spiritual animal of the project.

Electric eels are creations of the raw feelings a developer has during vibe-coding sessions — the current that runs through a person building alone at night. The FEEL documentation core is the **nest**. Skills are **eels spawned by the nest**: they swim out, connect to a user project's doc nest, and **electrify it**. Eels are social creatures; they bridge the human and the AI coder by carrying raw electrical power expressed as feelings.

## The public translation layer

What surfaces, and in what form. The cosmology stays here; the imagery travels.

| Lore element | Public-safe form | Where it may appear |
|---|---|---|
| Eels navigate by emitting weak electric fields and reading distortions (real biology: electrolocation) | **The one metaphor allowed in product copy:** *a FEEL head is a weak field a doc emits; the agent navigates by it without reading the body.* Explanatory, not mystical. | Landing page, README, talks |
| The nest and the spawned eels | Mascot + illustration system: eels, deep water, electric arcs. Visual only — never as terminology users must learn. | Logo, site art, slide decks |
| Electrifying a project's doc nest | Verb of adoption in marketing voice ("electrify your repo") | Taglines, launch posts |
| Raw feelings powering the eels | Stays private. | This file only |
| *feel of eel* origin story | One personal blog post, after the product has standing on its own merits — origin lore lands when there's already trust. | Blog, someday |
| Benchmark name | **eelbench** — playful, public-safe, zero cosmology required | Foundation docs, results posts |

## The hard line

1. **Product nouns stay boring.** Head, index, router, ceremony, skill. No user ever needs eel vocabulary to operate FEEL.
2. **Foundation docs are eel-free** (except the bench name). Rigor-side material reads as conservative engineering writing.
3. Precedent says mascots work when they decorate serious tech rather than explain it: DuckDB's duck, Go's gopher, Rust's crab, Docker's whale. The eel decorates; the docs convince.
